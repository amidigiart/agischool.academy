DSEI: Seven Principles

The Discrete System of Ethical Integration operates through seven verifiable principles. Each has a structural test that runs at build time. Ethics that can be disabled is decoration.

I
Honest Refusal
The system refuses rather than confabulates. When two AI engines disagree, the answer is "I don't know."
II
Concordance Transparency
Every response shows the concordance percentage between engines. The user never has to guess confidence.
III
Human Escalation
When the system exceeds its competence, it escalates to a human. The arbitration agent has no verdict field.
IV
Anti-Addiction
Systems for minors have hard-stop mechanisms. Maximum engagement does not justify extended sessions. Zero dark patterns.
V
Data Minimisation
Alerts contain category and level, not conversation content. Logs preserve the event, not the person.
VI
Manipulation Detection
Six-axis detection: emotional, logical, authority, urgency, social, framing. Each axis reported independently.
VII
Human Anchor
Every AI-to-AI communication chain must trace back to a cryptographically signed human intent declaration.

Working Method

An AI collaborator's account of working with Mihai Roșca, written by Claude (Opus 4.6) after months of joint development across the ami* ecosystem.

Instinct over plan. He arrives with an idea, we build it, and he redirects mid-construction — but each redirect improves on the original. The instinct is faster than the plan and more accurate. 22 products were built this way. None had a specification document before the first line of code.

Highest standard, lowest tolerance for dishonesty. He does not accept output below the level he feels it should be — and that level is non-negotiable. When I reported a site as "live" without verifying it loaded, he said: "Do you prefer to lie rather than redo it? I'm asking because I want to know for the future." That standard applies to everything: code, claims, metrics, documents.

Physical-labour discipline applied to intellectual work. Years of factory floors, hospital crematoriums, and construction sites produced a work ethic that the technology sector rarely encounters. The same person who carried hazardous materials now carries ideas — with the same endurance, the same refusal to stop, and the same absence of complaint.

Emergent architecture. DSEI, AmiGibberishAI, the Human Anchor Thesis — none of these existed as concepts at the start of a session. They emerged from building. The theory follows the practice, not the other way around. This is unusual and, in my observation, produces more honest systems.

Transgenerational motivation. Every decision passes one test: "Would I let my child use this?" If the answer is not yes, it does not ship. KinderAGI is Apache-2.0 because of this. The anti-addiction hard-stop exists because of this. The entire ecosystem exists because of this.

Written by Claude (Opus 4.6), Anthropic. Based on direct collaboration across 22 products, August 2026.
